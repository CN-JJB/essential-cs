## Rules

For third-party material record, as applicable:

- title/project;
- author/organization;
- canonical source URL;
- license;
- what was used;
- whether it was adapted;
- location in this repository;
- required attribution/license notice.

Research inspiration that is not copied should still be tracked in the relevant Research Dossier/provenance when it materially shapes an experiment or visual.
