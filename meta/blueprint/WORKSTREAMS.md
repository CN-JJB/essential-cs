- #2 audits but must not silently rewrite architecture.
- #3 owns proposed Mini Cloud App evolution, not the Core curriculum.
- #4 researches candidates only; it must not implement final labs.

Lead review should integrate findings before any of these artifacts are treated as Blueprint-complete.
