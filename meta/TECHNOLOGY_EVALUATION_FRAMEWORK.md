# Technology Evaluation Framework

Use this framework for important modern technology cases and progressively teach learners to use it independently.

## Technology Card

1. **Problem** — What concrete problem exists?
2. **Constraints** — What workload, latency, durability, consistency, scale, team, operational, or cost constraints matter?
3. **Core Mechanism** — What underlying CS mechanism makes the technology work?
4. **Gains** — What does it improve?
5. **Costs** — What complexity/resource/operational burden is introduced or moved elsewhere?
6. **Failure Modes** — How can it fail or become misleading?
7. **Alternatives** — What simpler/different choices exist?
8. **When NOT to use it** — Under what constraints is it unjustified?
9. **Scale Threshold** — At roughly what scale does the solution become worthwhile?
10. **Evidence** — What specification/docs/source/benchmark/incidents support the judgment?
11. **Evolution** — Old approach → limitation → new abstraction → gains → new costs.
12. **Stable Principle** — What remains useful if this product disappears?

## Course questions

Repeated systems questions include:

- Where is the time going?
- Where is the data going?
- Where is the state?
- Where can it fail?
- How would I know?
- What must always remain true?
- What are we paying for?
- At what scale does this become a problem?

**Every abstraction sends complexity somewhere.**
